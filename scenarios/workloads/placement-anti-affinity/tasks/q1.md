Deployment `${Q1_APP}` in namespace `${Q1_NS}` runs `2` replicas, and both sit on the same worker, so losing that node takes the whole app down.

Change the Deployment so that the scheduler can never put two Pods of `${Q1_APP}` on the same node, and make both replicas run. There may be more than one problem.

Do not delete or recreate the Deployment, and keep the image and the replica count.
