- It carries the label `tier=${Q3_LABEL_TIER}`.
- Create a PersistentVolumeClaim `${Q3_NAME}-claim` in namespace `${Q3_CLAIM_NS}` that binds to `${Q3_NAME}` and requests all of it.
- The volume only exists on `${Q3_AFFINITY_NODE}`: give it a required node affinity on `kubernetes.io/hostname`.
