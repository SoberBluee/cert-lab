Deployment `${Q2_APP}` in namespace `${Q2_NS}` runs `2` replicas behind Service `${Q2_APP}`. The team wants to take a Pod out of rotation for debugging without killing it.

1. Add a readiness probe to its container that runs the command `test ! -e /tmp/drain` every `2` seconds, so that one failure marks the Pod not ready.

2. Then take exactly one Pod out of the Service by creating the file `/tmp/drain` in it. Leave it that way: that Pod stays running but not ready, and the other Pod keeps serving.
