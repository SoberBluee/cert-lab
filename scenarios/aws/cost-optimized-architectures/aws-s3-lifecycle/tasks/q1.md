The bucket `logs-${SUFFIX}` keeps every application log forever in S3 Standard, and the bill keeps growing. Logs are read often in their first weeks, rarely after that, and must be kept for a while for audits.

Add a lifecycle rule with the ID `logs-tiering`, enabled, for objects under the prefix `logs/`:

- move objects to **S3 Standard-IA** after **${IA_DAYS}** days
- move them to **S3 Glacier Flexible Retrieval** after **${GLACIER_DAYS}** days
- delete them after **${EXPIRE_DAYS}** days
