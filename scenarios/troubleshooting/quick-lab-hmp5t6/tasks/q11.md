Use context: `kubectl config use-context k8s-beta`

`/opt/course/kustomize/${Q11_APP}-agent` on `controlplane` holds a node agent: a Namespace, a DaemonSet and their `kustomization.yaml`. Install it with `kubectl apply -k`, so that the DaemonSet runs a ready Pod on **every** node, the control plane included.

Get there with a patch in `/opt/course/kustomize/${Q11_APP}-agent/kustomization.yaml`. Do not edit `daemonset.yaml` or `namespace.yaml`, and do not change any node or its taints.
