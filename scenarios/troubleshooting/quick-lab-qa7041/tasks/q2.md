Use context: `kubectl config use-context kubernetes-admin@kubernetes`

1. Create a StorageClass `${Q2_NAME}` for statically provisioned volumes (provisioner `kubernetes.io/no-provisioner`) with reclaim policy `${Q2_RECLAIM}` and volume binding mode `${Q2_BINDING}`.

2. Then make `${Q2_NAME}` the default StorageClass of the cluster.

3. Then switch `${Q2_NAME}` from volume binding mode `${Q2_BINDING}` to the other mode. It must keep its name and every other setting.
