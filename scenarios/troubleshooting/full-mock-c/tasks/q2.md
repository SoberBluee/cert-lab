Use context: `kubectl config use-context k8s-ops`

Create a ServiceAccount `${Q2_SA}` in namespace `${Q2_NS}`, with the permissions below and nothing more.

- It may `get`, `list` and `watch` `${Q2_RESOURCE}` in `${Q2_NS}`.
- It may not write `${Q2_RESOURCE}`.
- It may not read any other resource, nor `${Q2_RESOURCE}` in other namespaces.
