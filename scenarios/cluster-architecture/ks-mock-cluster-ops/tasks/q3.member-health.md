Use context: `kubectl config use-context kubernetes-admin@kubernetes`

Inspect the cluster's etcd, which runs on `controlplane`, with `etcdctl` and write the results on `controlplane`:

1. `/opt/course/q3/members.txt`: the member list of the etcd cluster, including member IDs and names.
2. `/opt/course/q3/health.txt`: the health of the etcd endpoint.
3. `/opt/course/q3/version.txt`: the version of the etcd **server** that is running.

Do not change anything.
