Use context: `kubectl config use-context k8s-data`

Pod `${Q10_CLAIM}-app` in namespace `${Q10_NS}` never starts. Get it `Running` with PersistentVolumeClaim `${Q10_CLAIM}` mounted at `/data`, and that claim's volume provisioned by StorageClass `${Q10_CLAIM}-local`.

You may delete and re-create the Pod, the claim and the StorageClass, keeping their names and the Pod's image and command. Do not make any StorageClass the cluster default.
