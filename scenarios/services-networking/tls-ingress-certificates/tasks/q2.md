`https://${Q2_SUB}.${Q2_NS}.lab:30443/` answers, but clients receive Traefik's default certificate instead of the site's. The Ingress is `web` in namespace `${Q2_NS}`. The site's certificate and key are kept in Secret `site-tls` in namespace `cert-store`.

Make the site serve that certificate.

Do not change or delete the Secret in `cert-store`, and do not create a new certificate. You can see what is served with:

```bash
openssl s_client -servername ${Q2_SUB}.${Q2_NS}.lab -connect controlplane:30443 </dev/null 2>/dev/null | openssl x509 -noout -subject
```
